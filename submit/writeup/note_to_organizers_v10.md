What changed relative to v7:
- The segmentation ensemble is now the configuration selected by greedy forward selection plus local
  search on our 5-fold cross-validation with the official Dice and normalised Hausdorff distance:
  7 recipes, each entering with weight 1; seed replicas of a recipe are averaged inside that recipe's slot
  (25 checkpoints in total); five of the seven recipes use horizontal-flip test-time augmentation.
- The Task 3 head is retrained on features generated by the same code that runs in the container
  (a train/inference feature mismatch in v6/v7 was fixed).
- Runtime on our GPU for 139 frames is about 42 minutes; the container also measures its
  own throughput and reduces the ensemble automatically if the hardware is slower.
